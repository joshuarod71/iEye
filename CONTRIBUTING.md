# Contributing to iEye 👁️

First off, thank you for standing up to witness. iEye is a mission-driven protocol, and we are glad you're here to help build the "New True Free Press."

## Our Philosophy
We are building a lean, serverless, and un-censorable utility. We value:
* **Privacy over Profit:** No tracking, no accounts.
* **Clean Code:** If a human can't read it, the "system" can hide in it.
* **Radical Accountability:** We build the tools that let the public see the truth.

## How You Can Help
You don't need to be a master coder to contribute. We need help with:
* **The Core:** Keeping the JS footprint under 50KB.
* **The Mesh:** Optimizing WebRTC and DHT for high-traffic events.
* **The Ledger:** Ensuring IPFS/Arweave storage remains decentralized.
* **Documentation:** Making the "Poop Protocol" easy for everyone to understand.

## Ground Rules
1. **No Backdoors:** All Pull Requests will be audited by AI for hidden data-leaks.
2. **The "Poop" Standard:** We do not negotiate on the 30-day "Bullshit" deadline. It is the heart of the protocol.
3. **Stay Classy:** We are a global community. Respect the witnesses.

## Your First Step
Look at the [Issues] tab for anything labeled `good-first-issue` or `help-wanted`. If you have a big idea, open a new Issue to discuss it with the community first.
